About Charisbreed Excellence Academy

Our Story, Mission & People

Who We Are

Charisbreed Excellence Academy is a Nigerian private school located in Rumuowha, Eneka, Rivers State. We offer quality education from Early Years through Primary to Junior High School.

Our school combines the Nigerian, British and Montessori curricula to give every child a strong academic foundation, global exposure and good character.

We believe every child is unique and capable of excellence when given the right environment, guidance and support.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of learning where every child shines with excellence and becomes a leader for the future.

Our Mission

At Charisbreed Excellence Academy, we nurture creativity, build strong character, and model excellence in everything we do.

Our Values

Excellence, Integrity, Respect, Creativity and Discipline guide everything we do at Charisbreed.

Why Choose Charisbreed?

Strong Academic Foundation

We blend Nigerian, British and Montessori approaches so our learners are well prepared for both local and international opportunities.

Character Development

We deliberately teach values such as honesty, respect, responsibility and hard work alongside academic subjects.

Small Class Sizes

Our small classes allow teachers to give each child personal attention and support their individual learning needs.

Safe & Caring Environment

We provide a warm, secure and friendly environment where children feel happy, valued and motivated to learn.
