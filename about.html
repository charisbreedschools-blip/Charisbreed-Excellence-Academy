About Charisbreed Excellence Academy

Our Story, Mission & People

Who We Are

Charisbreed Excellence Academy is a Nigerian private school offering Early Years, Primary and Junior High School education in Rumuowha, Eneka, Rivers State.

We blend the Nigerian, British and Montessori curricula to give every child a rounded, globally competitive education while staying rooted in strong values and character.

Our Mission

To nurture confident, creative and disciplined learners through a balanced Nigerian, British and Montessori education.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of academic excellence and character development in Rivers State and beyond.

Our Values

Excellence, Integrity, Respect, Creativity and Discipline guide everything we do.
